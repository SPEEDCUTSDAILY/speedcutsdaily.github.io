Privacy Policy

SpeedCuts Uploader · effective September 23, 2026

HomeTerms of Service

SpeedCuts Uploader ("the tool") is a private, single-user tool that uploads video clips to one TikTok account, @speedcutsdaily, on the instruction of that account's owner. This policy explains what the tool handles and why.

Who uses the tool

Only the owner of the @speedcutsdaily account. The tool is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information from anyone else.

What information the tool handles

  • TikTok sign-in tokens. When the owner connects the account through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token. The tool stores them on the owner's own computer so it can upload approved clips.
  • Basic account information from TikTok (the account's open ID, display name and profile picture), shown in the tool so the owner can confirm which account a clip will post to.
  • Clip details the owner enters or approves: the video file, its caption, and the privacy, comment, Duet and Stitch settings chosen for the post.

How it is used

Solely to show the owner a preview of each post and to upload the clips the owner approves to the @speedcutsdaily account through TikTok's Content Posting API. The tool does not read the account's followers, messages or analytics.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone. Information goes only to TikTok, as part of uploading an approved clip.

Storage and security

Tokens and settings are kept on the owner's own computer, not on any server. They are never published.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the tool, and are then deleted. The owner can also revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's settings, under Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than the account owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Send a direct message to @speedcutsdaily on TikTok.

SpeedCuts Uploader is not affiliated with TikTok or IShowSpeed.
