SpeedCuts Uploader

The publishing tool behind the @speedcutsdaily TikTok fan page.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

SpeedCuts Uploader is a personal tool used by one person: the owner of the @speedcutsdaily TikTok account. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it.

What it does

  1. After IShowSpeed finishes a public YouTube live stream, the owner's software finds funny moments and edits them into short vertical clips with captions.
  2. The owner reviews each clip in the tool: a preview, the caption, the account it will post to, and a privacy setting the owner chooses.
  3. Only when the owner approves a clip does the tool upload it to the @speedcutsdaily account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Credit

Every clip comes from IShowSpeed's public live streams and is credited to him. @speedcutsdaily is a fan page. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or run by IShowSpeed or TikTok.

Contact

Send a direct message to @speedcutsdaily on TikTok. Requests to remove a clip are honoured.
